Greece's first civic climate corps. Paid service programs for 18–30 year olds. Dorida pilot restored trail networks to reconnect isolated Peloponnesian villages and seed ecotourism.

🥾 Paths of Greece

Developed 2,000+ km of hiking trails across Greece, many in the Peloponnese (Mani, Messenia, Kythera) — turning neglected footpaths into green economic infrastructure.

☀️ Energy cooperatives

Law 5037/2023 enables Renewable Energy Communities. The Peloponnese has abundant solar resource, but needs organizational capacity to launch rural cooperatives.
